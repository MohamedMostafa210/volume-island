# Install VolumeIsland (Phase 1 test)

## Get the file
1. On your PC, open https://github.com/MohamedMostafa210/volume-island/actions
2. Click the newest run with a green check.
3. Under **Artifacts**, click **VolumeIsland-ipa**. A zip downloads.
4. Unzip it. You now have `VolumeIsland.ipa`.

## Install with Sideloadly
1. Install Sideloadly from sideloadly.io and iTunes from apple.com (not the Microsoft Store versions).
2. On the iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode > On. It restarts.
3. Plug the iPhone into the PC and tap Trust.
4. Open Sideloadly. Drag `VolumeIsland.ipa` into it. Enter your Apple ID. Click Start.
5. On the iPhone: Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > your Apple ID > Trust.
6. Open VolumeIsland and tap Start.

Re-install the same way every 7 days.

## Test checklist
Play music in another app, then lock and unlock or go to the Home Screen so the Dynamic Island shows.
- [ ] Long-press the Dynamic Island, tap **+**: volume goes up
- [ ] Tap **-**: volume goes down
- [ ] Tap the speaker: volume mutes, tap again: it restores

If something fails, open VolumeIsland and read the red text on the screen.
